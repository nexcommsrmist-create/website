7YUVA TECH FEST — FLAGSHIP EVENT

ANTENNA DUEL Simulation Shootout

A high-intensity, real-time antenna simulation competition that challenges participants to design and optimize microstrip patch antennas under time pressure using CST Studio Suite.

Event Date: Will be Announce soon
Mode of Conduct: Offline (Computer Lab)
Registration Fee: ₹ 500/- per team
Team Size: Minimum 2 | Maximum 3
Duration: 6 Hours (Reporting: 9:00 AM)
Prize Pool: ₹ 7,000 (1st: ₹4k | 2nd: ₹2k | 3rd: ₹1k)

Inspired by industrial RF design sprints, teams are given an identical design brief and must race to achieve the best-performing antenna simulation. The competition culminates in a live re-optimization twist round that separates good engineers from great ones.

7YUVA TECH FEST — CLUB WORKSHOP

Antenna Design & RF Simulation using CST Studio Suite

A fully hands-on, practical workshop on microstrip antenna design and RF simulation using CST Studio Suite—one of the industry's leading 3D electromagnetic simulation tools. Conducted with technical support from Dassault Systèmes under SRM IST's existing MoU.

₹ 299/- Fee per participant
6 Hours Workshop Duration
Offline Mode of Conduct
e-Certificate Nationally Recognized

The session concludes with each participant completing a working 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi patch antenna simulation. Submitting the final simulation project screenshot qualifies participants for a workshop certificate.

OFFICIAL RULEBOOK

General Rules & Guidelines

Review the official YUVA Tech Fest rulebook parameters for both Antenna Duel and the CST Simulation Workshop.

1

Participants must carry a valid college ID proof and event registration/participation confirmation at all times during the fest.

2

All team members must report to the Computer Lab no later than 9:00 AM. Latecomers will forfeit their team’s slot without refund.

3

Participation is strictly open to registered YUVA TECH FEST 2026 attendees only. No walk-in entries will be accepted on the event day.

4

All design work must be performed live on the provided workstation systems during the event. Pre-built or pre-optimized CST project files brought from outside are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disqualification.

5

Each team is permitted to use only one workstation. Sharing of design files, screenshots, or simulation results between teams during the competition constitutes cheating and will lead to disqualification of all involved teams.

6

Any form of verbal, written, or digital communication regarding design parameters or results between teams during live rounds is strictly prohibited.

1

Participants must be seated and ready in the Computer Lab at least 15 minutes before the workshop start time (by 9:15 AM). The session will begin promptly at 9:30 AM.

2

All participants must use only the CST Studio project template shared by the instructor at the start of the session. Independently constructed or pre-built antenna projects are not permitted as starting points.

3

This is a 100% live simulation session. Participants are expected to follow and execute every step in real time alongside the instructor. Skipping steps or using pre-completed stages is not permitted.

4

A mandatory mid-session progress check will be conducted at approximately the 3-hour mark. Participants must demonstrate a functional antenna model in CST with S-parameter plots to remain eligible for the certificate.

5

Full and uninterrupted attendance for the entire 6-hour session is compulsory to qualify for the nationally recognized workshop e-certificate. Participants who leave before the session ends will not be eligible.

6

At the conclusion of the workshop, participants must export their final simulation result (screenshot of S11 plot and 3D far-field pattern) and submit it through the official Google Form shared by the coordinator.

WHAT IS NEX-COMM?

Explore communication technology beyond classroom theory.

Learn. Build. Research. Compete. We are a student-driven ecosystem where you simulate, experiment, build, and create practical technical solutions.

Antennas & RF

Design microstrip antennas, model RF systems, and master simulation software like CST Studio Suite and HFSS.

Wireless & 5G/6G

Dive into the future of network connectivity, analyzing beamforming, phased arrays, and next-generation transmission networks.

Satellite & Space Comm

Understand orbital links, CubeSat communication modules, and Doppler shift effects on space telemetry.

OUR PROCESS

Learn. Simulate. Build. Test. Compete.

We don't just learn theory. We put it into practice. Discover your direction from fundamentals to hardware prototypes.

Antennas & RF Design

From a concept on paper to a working hardware prototype. Master the foundation of modern wireless technology.

Key Modules Covered:

  • Learn: RF fundamentals and antenna parameters.
  • Simulate: Design using CST Studio Suite, HFSS, and MATLAB.
  • Build: Fabricate microstrip patch antennas and RF components.
  • Test: Take real-world measurements using VNAs and S-Parameters.

Signals & SDR

What happens behind every wireless connection? Analyze and manipulate the invisible waves that connect our devices.

Key Modules Covered:

  • Signal Processing: Digital signal processing and waveform analysis.
  • SDR (Software Defined Radio): Experimenting with live radio frequencies.
  • Embedded DSP: Implementing algorithms on hardware.
  • Network Analysis: Exploring how devices, antennas, and networks communicate.

Projects & Competitions

Take your ideas further through technical projects, research, and high-stakes competitions like Antenna DUEL.

Opportunities:

  • Technical Competitions: Apply knowledge under real constraints.
  • Hands-On Workshops: Real-world design and simulation tutorials.
  • Research: Prepare for higher studies with documentation and publications.
  • Hardware Systems: Build and showcase your own ECE prototypes.

Wireless Transmission & Modulation

Study the physical communication systems that link bio-sensors to hospital monitors. Model RF path loss, analyze modulation schemes, and evaluate signal attenuation under ambient noise.

Key Modules Covered:

  • RF Telemetry Modulation: Digital modulations (QPSK, FSK, QAM) under AWGN noise.
  • Antenna Parameters: Simulating radiation patterns, return loss, Smith charts, and VSWR.
  • Link Budget Analysis: Path loss models and receiver noise margins.
  • Error Control: Hamming codes and cyclic redundancy checks (CRC).

HDL Modeling & Hardware Co-Processors

Translate filtering mathematical equations into RTL code. Implement custom digital signal processors directly on silicon wafers, analyzing gate count, latency, and power efficiency.

Key Modules Covered:

  • RTL Hardware Synthesis: SystemVerilog modeling of logic arrays and FIR filters.
  • Silicon Layout constraints: Wafer routing, CMOS gate delay, parasitical extractions.
  • Error Metrics: Testing hardware layouts for bit error rates (BER).
  • Verification: Design Rule Checking (DRC) and layout vs. schematic (LVS).
